We’re working to change the course of T1D.

Today’s treatments focus on replacing the insulin your body can no longer make. That’s essential, but insulin therapies and delivery devices cannot match your body’s natural ability to regulate glucose, and they do not stop the immune attack that destroys your insulin-producing cells in the first place.

T1D affects millions of people worldwide. In the U.S., approximately 2 million people are living with insulin-dependent, symptomatic T1D (Stage 3 and beyond), with roughly 64,000 new diagnoses each year.
